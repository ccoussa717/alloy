# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Alloy a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness.
- Respect differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Take responsibility, apologize, and learn from mistakes.
- Focus on what is best for the community and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, or personal attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Enforcement

Report sensitive conduct concerns through GitHub's private
[Report Abuse](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse) process and
include links to the relevant repository content. Do not use public issues for
sensitive reports. Maintainers may remove content, reject contributions, issue
warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant
when warranted.

Anyone responsible for enforcing this policy must recuse themselves from a
report involving them; GitHub Support remains the independent escalation path.
